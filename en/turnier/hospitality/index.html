Hospitality

Free accommodation for all main-draw players.

The tournament provides free accommodation with breakfast to all main-draw players. Hospitality begins the day before the start of the second round of the main draw and ends the day after elimination. Only twin rooms are available, shared with another main-draw player. Coaches, qualifying players, family members and other companions are not included in the package and book directly with the hotel at their own cost.
