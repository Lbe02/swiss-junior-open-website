Switzerland's top-rated junior tournament.

The Swiss Junior Open 2026 is an international junior tennis tournament in the J200 category of the World Tennis Junior Tour. After the four Grand Slams, it is the third-highest category on the tour.

A springboard to the big stage.

For many players, Basel is the last stop before professional tennis. Winners here often appear on the ATP and WTA rankings just a few years later.

As Switzerland's top-rated junior tournament, it brings 128 of the best junior players from around the world to Basel every year, and ranks among the 50 largest clay-court tournaments on the World Tennis Junior Tour.

A week of world-class junior tennis, right in the region: free admission, open courts, top-level sport up close.
